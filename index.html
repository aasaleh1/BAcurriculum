Teacher plans and proposals

Anything marked as a teacher's own comes from their plan for this year. Anything marked proposed was drafted for review and still needs confirming before it counts.

One dataset behind everything

Every guide is rebuilt from a single dataset, so two pages cannot disagree. Send corrections to Aamena. Edits made on a page itself are lost at the next build.

Quarters are colored, not subjects

The four quarter colors run the same way on every page, so a glance tells you where in the year you are rather than which subject you are looking at.

Built from the teachers' own plans and the Florida standards for each subject: B.E.S.T. for English and mathematics, NGSSS for science and social studies, and the department plans for Qur’an, Tajweed, Arabic and Islamic Studies. Writing units, activities, vocabulary and poetry sets were drafted for teacher review and are marked as proposals wherever they appear.
