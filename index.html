How to read these

Three conventions run through every page.

Where a decision came from

Units and benchmarks are marked Teacher when they come from the teacher's own annual plan, Approved when the curriculum office signed them off, and Proposed when they are still a draft awaiting confirmation.

One source behind everything

Every guide is generated from the subject workbooks in the shared drive, so no two pages can disagree. Corrections go to the curriculum office; an edit made on a page itself is lost at the next build.

The quarter in progress

The current quarter is marked in the header of every year map, counted against the school calendar rather than the raw date range, so the Ramadan and Eid break is not counted as instructional time.

Bayaan Academy
